# Decision map format

Scale the map to the stakes. A quick map may use one sentence per field; a full review should show the evidence and sensitivity needed to audit the recommendation.

## Decision

- **Decision-maker:** Who controls the immediate move?
- **Decision:** What must be chosen?
- **Deadline and horizon:** When is the move made, and how long do consequences matter?
- **Objective:** What outcome is being optimized?
- **Constraints:** What cannot be traded away?
- **Default:** What happens without action?

Do not fill the table for decoration. Include only claims that affect the model or recommendation.

## Actors and incentives

| Actor | Objective | Moves and authority | Constraints | Outside option | Information |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  |  |  |  |  |  |

Call out internal agents, veto holders, implementers, and coalitions when they do not share one payoff.

## Interaction

- **Relevant game shapes:** Name and justify them.
- **Sequence:** What happens first, what is observed, and who responds?
- **Best responses:** What is each actor likely to do after each serious move?
- **Commitments and signals:** Which are credible, and why?
- **Power and institutions:** What can override modeled incentives?
- **Second-order effects:** What precedent, reputation, learning, or coalition change follows?

Use a payoff table or decision tree only when it preserves the important response structure.

Remove rows that are not credible and add materially different moves when needed.

## Recommendation

- **Classification:** Robust move, contingent move, judgment call, or challenge.
- **Next move:** One executable action.
- **Owner and timing:** Who acts by when?
- **Expected observable response:** What should happen if the model is right?
- **Stop condition:** What prevents escalation or sunk-cost persistence?
- **Next decision point:** When will the strategy be revisited?
- **Why this move:** Explain the incentive logic and option value.

## Sensitivity

- **Flip conditions:** Facts or assumptions that would change the recommendation.
- **Early indicators:** Observable signs that reveal which game is being played.
- **Residual uncertainty:** What the model cannot resolve.
