# SOC 2 source authority and evidence limits

Before a formal mapping, verify current editions and the service auditor's requirements against authorized AICPA sources:

- [AICPA SOC suite](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/landing/system-and-organization-controls-soc-suite-of-services)
- [Trust Services Criteria](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/download/2017-trust-services-criteria-with-revised-points-of-focus-2022)
- [SOC 2 Description Criteria](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/download/get-description-criteria-for-your-organizations-soc-2-r-report)
- [Currently effective SSAEs](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/download/aicpa-ssaes-currently-effective)

Implementation and assessment guidance can come from [NIST SP 800-53](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/r5/upd1/final), [SP 800-53A](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/a/r5/final), [CSA Cloud Controls Matrix](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/cloud-controls-matrix), [OWASP ASVS](https://owasp.org/www-project-application-security-verification-standard/), and [MITRE ATT&CK](https://attack.mitre.org/). They help design and challenge controls; they do not replace AICPA criteria or constitute evidence of operation.

The package contains original workflow guidance and a blank discovery register. It does not redistribute licensed AICPA manuals, private security libraries, customer evidence or source datasets. A configured corpus is optional: cite its underlying source, edition and locator, distinguish retrieved guidance from observed operation, and verify currentness before issuing consequential conclusions. An inaccessible standard is a source gap, not permission to invent criterion text.
